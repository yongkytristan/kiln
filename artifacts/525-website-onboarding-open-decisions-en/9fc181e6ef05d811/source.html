1. Purpose and scope

This page covers the website onboarding plan for #525: the Atlas identifiers, the new parts and the end-to-end flow (sections 3 and 4), the open items D1, D5, D6, and D7 (sections 5 to 9), and the RFC question raised by #549 (section 14). It does not repeat D2–D4 or the decided items. The Indonesian version covers the full plan, including D2–D4 and the decided items.

2. Atlas identifiers

Atlas issues a discovery_id when a check is run first, and an acquisition_id for the import. Arcane creates the submission_id and reuses it for resubmits within 24 hours. Results are read by acquisition_id. The context_id is an HMAC over the user and attempt ID, so it stays stable across polling, retries, and app creation. The attestation value is part of the request identity, so it must also be stored.

6. D5 Current state of MI-1

The backend design covers two cases: capacity full (resubmit the same submission_id after Atlas's retry_after_seconds) and expiry (silent re-check of a discovery, a 24-hour draft, a 30-day result).

In the MI-1 design, pages 2, 4, and 6 could not be read, and pages 8 to 22 have not been read. Pages 1, 3, 5, and 7 do not show screens for an empty site, capacity full, Atlas unavailable, a rejected token, or expired results. So it is not confirmed whether MI-1 defines them at all.

MI-1 already defines these screens: site refuses reads, site not found, robots.txt disallows, import failed (with "Try again" and "Continue without website").

8. D6 Page and product limits

Facts.

  • Website page and depth limits are set in the Atlas deployment, not in the contract. A CLI capture recorded 10 pages (atlas#18). A live capture of a demo site recorded 50 pages with stopped_by: max_pages (deployment runbook §9). The production limit is not confirmed.
  • Capture duration (300 seconds in the CLI) is separate from the 15-minute operation deadline. They are not compared.
  • Website contract: website-acquisition-execution-v1 §3.1 allows the first HTTP binding to ignore a tighter limit from Arcane. So Arcane may not be able to request fewer pages. The deployed binding has not been checked.
  • Marketplace: Atlas reads up to 2,000 products today. The ecommerce v1 contract has no caller limit field (arcane#549).

Proposal for marketplace: Atlas's limit stays fixed. Arcane enforces its own limit when the merchant picks products (step 5b on the canvas). Provisional limit: 2,000 products, 15 images per product (the Dewastore importer's limit).

Open: whether Atlas adds a limit field (atlas#19 EA-5, atlas#22); the production page limit for websites; whether website and marketplace limits differ (arcane#549).

Impact: wait time (Atlas accepts four concurrent acquisitions across callers, retry after 30 seconds) and image storage.

9. D7 Atlas credentials

  • The contract splits two capabilities, each with its own scope, Authentik app, and hostname:
    • Website: website-acquisition-execution-v1, scope atlas:website-acquisition, app atlas-website-acquisition.
    • Ecommerce: ecommerce-acquisition-v1, scope atlas:ecommerce-acquisition, app atlas-ecommerce-acquisition.
  • Arcane stores the client ID and secret in its secret store. The Atlas registration command does not take the secret.
  • A client maps to an integration identity (--integration arcane, one per environment). During rotation, two registrations may map to the same integration.
  • Revocation blocks new calls, including reads, even when the token has not expired. Rotation does not reset the deduplication windows.

Proposal: two clients, following the scope and app split. One client with both scopes has not been checked in Authentik [INFERENCE].

Open: whether a production credential is already registered. A runbook note mentions a "first live credential, #13"; this has not been verified.

Status: needs discussion, tied to AT-1 and the Atlas operator.

14. RFC #549 Marketplace catalogue before the app exists

Use case

A merchant who sells on Tokopedia or Shopee pastes a shop link. Atlas reads the catalogue, and the merchant reviews it. Arcane builds a shop from the selected products and guides the merchant into the FDE program. Acceptance in #549: merchants get into the FDE program.

Problem

Between Import and confirm, Arcane must hold a catalogue of up to 2,000 products, each with up to 15 images, and keep it through reloads, closed tabs, and lost responses. No app exists yet, so app-scoped storage (apps.requirements) is not available. The browser draft lives in one browser and expires after 24 hours. Its capacity for this data is not verified [INFERENCE]. The current design has no place for pre-app product rows.

Current state

  • The attempt and draft are in browser localStorage, keyed dewabuilder.onboarding.draft.<attempt_id> (main at 3c01f7d2). The server has no record for an attempt without an app.
  • Images are copied into user-owned assets before Review, with no stub app. Jonathan agreed this on 6 November. Assets outlive their origin app (ADR-067).
  • Products: Jonathan chose generated-app data files for the first release, with no product database. Every selected product is persisted, even when generation receives only a bounded sample.
  • The Atlas context_id is app-less and stable (agreed 6 November).
  • Not yet specified: Arcane's owned mapping and persistence for the pre-app context, including reload and resume; where the complete pre-app catalogue is kept; how the selection becomes generated-app data files.
  • Dewastore's import worker stores products in its own database. This is a behavior reference, not a schema to copy. Dewastore is being retired.

Options

OptionDescriptionAdvantagesDisadvantages
A. Browser draft holds the catalogueNo server storageNo migrationOne browser, 24 hours. May not hold 2,000 products with metadata [INFERENCE]. Does not survive a lost browser.
B. Server-side pre-app import recordNew owner-scoped table with a catalogue snapshot per attempt, with expiry and cleanupSurvives reload, lost response, and device change. Owner-scoped.Migration. New lifecycle and cleanup. New pattern for pre-app data.
C. User-owned file referenced by attemptCatalogue snapshot stored as a user-owned file, reusing asset storageNo new tableNot checked whether the assets model accepts a non-image file, or how unconfirmed attempts are cleaned up [INFERENCE].

Decision needed

No option is chosen here. Jonathan decides whether the RFC is opened and which option it examines. Evidence so far: option B adds a table, which means a database migration, and the repo's escalation rule requires a human decision before a migration. Option A has not been measured against 2,000 products with 15 images each across devices. Option C's cleanup is unverified. The same record could serve website D1 option (b), so one RFC would cover both.

Questions for the RFC

  1. Where is the pre-app catalogue kept, and for how long?
  2. Who owns it, and how is access scoped (user and attempt)?
  3. How do reload and lost-response retry resume?
  4. How does the final selection become generated-app data files at confirm?
  5. Does the website (D1 option b) use the same record?

Status: open. RFC not filed. The decision is Jonathan's. Not yet discussed.
